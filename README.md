## Overview

Organizations collect large volumes of employee attendance data every day, but raw attendance records alone provide limited business value. The challenge lies in transforming this data into meaningful workforce insights that support HR decision-making and operational planning.

This project focuses on analyzing employee attendance patterns using Power BI to provide visibility into workforce presence, work-from-home trends, and sick leave behavior.

The dashboard enables HR teams and business leaders to monitor workforce availability, identify attendance trends, and make informed planning decisions.

## Business Problem

The HR team needed a centralized reporting solution to answer important workforce-related questions such as:

* What is the overall employee presence percentage?
* How has attendance changed over time?
* Which days experience the highest absenteeism?
* How frequently are employees working from home?
* What are the trends in sick leave usage?
* How can workforce planning be improved during periods of lower attendance?

Without a structured analytics solution, identifying these patterns required manual analysis and significant effort.

## Business Metrics Used

### Presence %

Measures the percentage of working days where employees were present.

This KPI provides a high-level view of workforce availability.

### Work From Home Percentage (WFH %)

Measures the percentage of working days completed remotely.

This helps HR teams understand remote work adoption and employee preferences.

### Sick Leave Percentage (SL %)

Measures the percentage of working days lost due to illness.

This metric helps identify workforce health trends and operational risks.

## Solution

An interactive Power BI dashboard was developed to transform attendance records into actionable workforce insights.

The dashboard enables users to:

* Monitor workforce attendance performance
* Track work-from-home trends
* Analyze sick leave behavior
* Compare attendance patterns across employees
* Identify workforce availability trends
* Support capacity planning and operational decision-making

## Key Insights Generated

The dashboard helps uncover several workforce insights:

* Attendance patterns over time
* Work-from-home adoption trends
* Sick leave trends across reporting periods
* Day-wise workforce behavior
* Employee-level attendance performance
* Workforce capacity planning opportunities
